# THREADVIBE — Samrat Fashion Product Context

## Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Brand**: THREADVIBE by Samrat Fashion
- **Positioning**: Premium Indian men's streetwear, oversized apparel, and on-demand custom printing studio.
- **Core Aesthetic**: Contemporary urban streetwear, brutalist and editorial minimalism, Tokyo cyberpunk and underground graphic art.

## Core Audience & Market
- **Primary Audience**: Men aged 18–34 seeking structured heavyweight streetwear, oversized boxy fits, expressive anime/cyberpunk graphics, and premium minimalist blanks.
- **Geography**: Pan-India delivery via integrated Delhivery logistics network with express fulfillment from New Delhi.
- **Business Priority**: Men's streetwear & graphic tees. (Women's catalog routes and data preserved in backend without primary merchandising prominence).

## Product Categories
1. **Oversized Tees**: Heavyweight 240+ GSM 100% combed cotton jersey with engineered drop-shoulder patterns.
2. **Graphic Streetwear**: Underground street art, acid-wash vintage graphics, and urban typography.
3. **Anime & Cyberpunk**: Retro-futuristic Tokyo mecha, neon cyberpunk drops, and anime illustrations.
4. **Minimal Essentials**: Monochrome boxy blanks, terry cuts, and clean architectural silhouettes.
5. **Custom Printing Studio**: On-demand custom DTF apparel printing with front, back, and sleeve placements, live preview, and staff mockup verification.

## Authentic Value Propositions & Customer Promises
- **Material Quality**: 240+ GSM heavyweight 100% combed cotton jersey.
- **Wash Durability**: Pre-shrunk, bio-washed fabric with high-density Direct-to-Film (DTF) inks tested for 50+ washes.
- **Shipping Policy**: Complimentary express delivery across India on prepaid orders above ₹999.
- **Payment Security**: 100% encrypted checkout via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, and COD where serviceable).
- **Customer Assurance**: 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy with automated tax invoice generation.
